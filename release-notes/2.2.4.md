PC5 2.2.4 lets games see how much free space a drive has, so games that check it before saving can save.

### Fixes from your reports

- **Saving in games that check the free space first**: games couldn't see how big a drive is or how much room is left on it. A game that checks before it saves either refused to save or, like one that divided by the empty answer, crashed after a few minutes. Games now see the console's real size and free space.
