PC5 2.2.2-test is a test build of surround sound (5.1 and 7.1 for games that play it) and of a smoother start: it is everything in 2.2.2, plus a **Sound output** setting on each game, and the loading screen from Play until the game shows without the jump in between.

This is a pre-release for testing. PC5's own updater doesn't offer it, and it offers you the next normal release when there is one.

### Install

Download `PPSA11018.zip` below, unzip it and replace the `PPSA11018` folder on the console with the new one, the way you added PC5 (see [Installing and updating](https://github.com/bornaradusin/PC5/wiki/Installing-and-updating#by-hand)). Your games, saves and settings stay. To go back, do the same with 2.2.2's zip.

### What to test

- Press **△** on a game, then set **Sound output** to **5.1** or **7.1** (it's **Stereo** until you change it). Left and right go through the three.
- Play a scene you know well, with sound from behind and the sides: footsteps, gunfire, ambient sound. Does each sound come from the speaker it should? Is the centre (dialogue) in the middle and the sub working?
- If the game is silent, or sound only comes from the front, set it back to **Stereo** and tell us which game it was.
- Please report in the [surround sound discussion](https://github.com/bornaradusin/PC5/discussions/219): the game, 5.1 or 7.1, your speakers or receiver, and what you heard. If something sounds wrong, attach the game's newest `log-…zip` from `/data/prospero-win/logs/`.

### A smoother start

When you press Play, PC5's screen now fades to black, and the loading screen fades in from black as soon as the game can draw, staying until the game shows its first picture. Before, PC5 showed the loading picture, the screen went black for a moment, and then the loading picture came again. A short black moment right after Play stays: nothing can draw while the console switches from PC5 to the game.

Please tell us if a game doesn't start with this build that started with 2.2.2.

### How surround works

With 5.1 or 7.1, the game is told the console has that many speakers, and PC5 plays each of the game's channels on its own speaker instead of mixing everything down to two. The PS5 then sends the sound on the way it does for its own games, so it follows the PS5's own audio settings. If the console won't open surround, the game plays in stereo as before.
